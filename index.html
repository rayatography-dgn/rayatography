03 · ABOUT

More Than Just Making Things Look Good.

Design is fundamentally a communication mechanism. Before opening any design software or generating an asset, I analyze the underlying problem: What is the core message? Who is the audience? How will this visual piece shift perception and drive decisive action?

As a Creative Director and Brand Designer, I combine strategic thinking with meticulous art direction. From brand identity architecture and multi-channel campaign rollouts to concept-driven AI visuals and high-converting social storytelling, every visual element is created with purpose.
